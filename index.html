Privacy Policy · Terms

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Sync uses a secret vault code instead.

Where are my links stored?

On your phone. A synced copy only exists if you turn sync on.

How do I get costumes for Mr. Ink?

Save links and finish quests. Open the Wardrobe tab.

Do costumes cost anything?

No. They are earned, never sold.

Does the Wardrobe send anything to your server?

No. It stays on your phone.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone for now.

How do I save a link from another app?

Tap Share, then Inkling. Not there? Tap More and switch it on.

What if I lose my vault code?

We can't recover it. Your links stay on your phone, so export a backup now and then.

How do I delete my data?

Sync tab, then Delete synced copy. Deleting the app clears your phone.
