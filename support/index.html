Support

Happy to help.

Write to us at [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL]. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what happened. Never email your vault code unless we ask for it to fix a sync problem.

Inkling isn't in the share menu

Tap Share in the other app. In the row of apps, scroll to the end and tap More, then switch Inkling on. Tap Edit and the green + to pin it to the front. Some apps, such as Facebook, show their own share list first. Tap More there to reach the iPhone's share menu.

My saved link didn't show up

Links saved from the share menu are added when you open Inkling. Open the app and they appear within a moment.

Using Inkling on more than one device

Open the Sync tab. Your vault code is at the top. On your other device, open Sync, enter the code under "Add another device", and tap Connect to Vault. Keep the code private: anyone who has it can open your vault.

I lost my vault code

We can't recover it, because Inkling has no accounts and we don't know who owns a vault. Your links are still on your phone. Use Export Backup on the Sync tab to keep a copy.

Delete my data

On the Sync tab, tap Delete synced copy to remove your vault from our server. You can also switch Cloud sync off so your links never leave your phone. Deleting the app removes everything stored on it. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Where are Mr. Ink's costumes?

Open the Wardrobe tab. Costumes are earned by saving links and finishing quests, and Mr. Ink only wears what you choose. They are kept on your phone only, so deleting the app also removes them.

Something looks wrong

Try closing the app and opening it again. If it keeps happening, email us with a short description and, if you can, a screenshot.
