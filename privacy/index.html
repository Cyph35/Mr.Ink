Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-15 · Effective November 8, 2026

This policy explains what Inkling does with your information. In short: no account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics, and nothing is sold. [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY] is responsible for your data in the way described here.

1. What stays on your device

Your saved links, titles, notes, tags, folders, favorites and settings are stored on your device. When you use "Save to Inkling" from another app's share menu, the link waits in a private storage area shared only between Inkling and its share window on your device.

If you turn cloud sync off, nothing at all is sent to our server: your links stay on your device and the server's link previews are switched off too.

Previews from your iPhone: in the iPhone app, unless you turn it off in the Sync tab, your iPhone asks the website of each saved link for its title and picture, using Apple's own link preview tool, the same as opening the link would. This does not go through our server and works even when cloud sync is off. The pictures are kept only on your device and are not synced.

Costumes earned by saving links are kept only on your device, along with what Mr. Ink is wearing. They are never sent to our server, and there are no purchases: costumes cannot be bought.

2. What is sent to our server

Only when cloud sync is on, your saved links, titles, notes, tags, folders, favorite and pin marks, and the times you changed them, are sent to our server and stored there. They are identified only by your vault code. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number and do not link a vault to a person.

Link previews and pictures (only while cloud sync is on): when you save a link, its web address is sent to our server, which loads that page to read its title and picture and sends them back. Link pictures are also loaded by our server for you, so the website the link points to never sees your device. We do not store these addresses or pictures, although your device may keep a picture in its own cache.

Like any online service, our server (and the company that hosts it) can see the network address of each connection. We use it only to limit abuse (for example, too many requests), not to build a profile of you.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your information.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising or analytics tools.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not read your content, except automatically to provide the features above.

Fonts are included in the App, so no font company is contacted when you use it.

4. The clipboard

When you open Inkling it may check whether you copied a web link, only to offer to save it. What you copied stays on your device unless you choose to save it with cloud sync on.

5. Other websites

When previews from your iPhone are on, the websites of the links you save can see your network address when your iPhone asks them for a preview. Pictures from pages you save may also load directly from those websites, which can then see your network address. Opening a saved link takes you to that website, which has its own privacy policy.

6. How long we keep things, and how to delete them

A synced vault that is not used for 60 days is deleted from our server automatically. Links in the Trash are deleted after 30 days.

You can delete your synced copy yourself at any time: Sync screen, then "Delete synced copy". You can also turn sync off there. Deleting the App removes everything stored on your device.

To get a copy of your links, use "Export Backup" on the Sync screen.

7. How we protect your data

Your connection to our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Vault codes are long random codes. On our server, vault files are named with a one-way hash of the code, so the code itself is not written to disk. The server limits how fast anyone can try requests, and does not allow the preview feature to reach private networks.

No system is perfectly secure. Keep your vault code private, because it is the key to your vault.

8. Where your data is processed

Our server is run by [YOUR HOSTING PROVIDER AND SERVER REGION]. If you use Inkling from another country, your synced data is sent to and stored in that location.

9. Children

Inkling is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where the law sets that age), and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used sync, contact us and we will delete the vault.

10. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to or limit how it is used. Most of this you can do yourself in the App (export, delete synced copy, turn sync off).

Because vaults are not linked to a named person, we can act on a request only if you give us your vault code. Contact us at [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL].

If this policy changes in a way that matters, Inkling will ask you to review it again.
